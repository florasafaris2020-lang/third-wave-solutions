      What happens if you are interested

      1. Enquiry

      Send the form on this page or call. We confirm the business is still available and answer your first questions.

      2. Confidentiality

      You sign a short confidentiality undertaking, then receive the fuller profile, financial summary and licence schedule.

      3. Vetting

      You commission the vetting (see scope and fees). We verify cash flow, KRA status, licences and liabilities independently of the seller.

      4. Offer

      You decide with a written report in front of you. We can sit in on the negotiation if you want that support.
