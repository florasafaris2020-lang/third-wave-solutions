Salon & Beauty Spa — Kiambu Road

An eight-station salon and spa with a repeat clientele, a trained team and a lease in place. Asking price of KES 3.8M includes fittings, equipment and the client book.

Kiambu Road, Nairobi Revenue KES 145,000 / month 5 staff including 4 stylists Lease: 3 years remaining
The business

What you are buying

A neighbourhood salon and day spa on a busy stretch of Kiambu Road, trading since 2019. The business runs eight styling stations, two treatment rooms and a small retail shelf for take-home products. Revenue is built on a loyal repeat base — roughly 60% of weekly appointments come from clients who have visited more than six times — supported by walk-in traffic from the surrounding estates and offices.

Four stylists and one receptionist are employed, all on written contracts. The current owner manages bookings, supplier relationships and marketing, and is prepared to stay on for a short handover. The salon uses a booking system with client history, which transfers with the sale.

Why the owner is selling

The owner is relocating out of Nairobi and cannot run the salon remotely. They are willing to stay on for a handover period and to introduce the new owner to key suppliers and long-standing clients. The reason given should be tested alongside the numbers during vetting.
