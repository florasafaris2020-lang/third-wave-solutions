Café & Bistro — Westlands

A daytime café minutes from the Westlands office cluster, with a fitted kitchen, corporate lunch trade and two delivery accounts. Asking KES 5.2M.

Westlands, Nairobi Revenue KES 310,000 / month 9 staff Lease: 2 years remaining
The business

What you are buying

A 42-cover café and bistro trading seven days a week, with its strongest hours between 7am and 3pm. The menu is built around breakfast, coffee and a short bistro lunch list, which keeps kitchen staffing simple and food cost predictable. Two corporate delivery accounts and a growing takeaway trade provide weekday volume that is less exposed to weekend weather.

The kitchen was refitted two years ago and the equipment — ovens, refrigeration, coffee machine and extraction — is owned outright and included in the sale. Nine staff are employed across kitchen, front of house and cleaning, all with written contracts. The owner runs purchasing and the accounts.

Status as presented by the seller. Each item is independently confirmed during a vetting engagement.

  • Delivery platform contracts — commission terms to be reviewed in vetting

Included in the sale

  • Fully fitted kitchen, ovens, refrigeration and coffee machine
  • 42 covers of tables, chairs and counter fit-out
  • Brand name, menu, recipes and Google/social profiles
  • Supplier accounts and negotiated pricing
  • Stock at valuation, to be counted at completion
  • Landlord consent to assign the lease

Points a buyer should test

The 2023 result is thin and the recovery to 2025 depends on the corporate lunch accounts. A vetting should reconcile daily sales to bank and M-Pesa deposits, confirm that the rent is at market, check the lease assignment clause, and verify food cost against supplier invoices rather than the seller's summary.
