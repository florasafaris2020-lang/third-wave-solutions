Questions sellers ask

Before you list

Will my staff and competitors find out?

No. We do not publish your business name, address or photos without written consent. Buyers are screened and sign a confidentiality undertaking before they see financials or the exact location.

What if my records are incomplete?

Tell us. Missing M-Pesa records, untidy books or unfiled returns are common. We will tell you what to reconstruct before going to market, because a buyer's vetting will find it anyway.

Do I have to accept a vetting?

Buyers who are risking their savings will want independent checks, and we recommend they do. A business that can be verified sells faster and at a better price than one that cannot.

Can I sell shares instead of assets?

Both are possible, and the choice affects tax, licences and liabilities. We will flag the difference and ask you to take advice from your advocate and tax advisor before committing.
